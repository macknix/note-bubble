To swap any of them, keep the `pop-N` name — `PopSounds` looks up `mp3`, `m4a`,
`aiff`, `caf`, then `wav`. `Scripts/make-pop-sounds.py` regenerates synthesised
`.wav` fallbacks, which sit last in that order and so will not shadow these.
