Notes are bubbles that sit over whatever you're working on. They grow to fit what you write, pack themselves together, redden as they get old — and burst with a satisfying pop when the job is done.

Everything it does

A to-do list that
behaves like soap.

Rounded glass tiles, packed by masonry. Each one is measured to fit its text, drifts from green to red as it waits, and bursts with a sound when you're done.

●

Triage by colour

Every note shifts green → amber → red over a week, so what's gone stale is obvious without reading a word.

◎

Bubbles inside bubbles

Any note can hold sub-notes, arbitrarily deep. A badge says how many; the breadcrumb brings you back up.

⇆

Separate boards

Keep work, home and someday apart. Two-finger swipe between them, or drag a bubble onto a dot to file it there.

▭

Grows to fit

Tiles are measured to their text and packed by masonry — short notes tuck in beside tall ones. Wordy notes widen to two columns.

✦

Popping is the reward

Hold a bubble, watch it swell, and it bursts with one of five real pop sounds. ⌘Z if you were wrong.

◐

Out of the way

Rests in the menu bar and as a small pill. Hover to open, move away and it folds back. No Dock icon, nothing in ⌘-Tab.

The traffic lights

Stale things
turn red.

Every note is dated from when you wrote it, and its colour moves continuously — no buckets, just a hue sweeping from green to red. Each board can set its own week.

  • 🟢Fresh for the first three-sevenths of the window.
  • 🟠Ageing until it ends — amber halfway through.
  • ❗Overdue after: red, with a ! so it never relies on colour alone.
  • 〰Shake it to reset its age — it turns green in your hand.

Workspaces

Boards you
swipe between.

Two-finger swipe across the panel, the way you would between pages. The dots are the map: one per board, the one you're on drawn wider, each coloured by the worst thing on it.

  • ⇆Swipe, ⌘← / ⌘→, or click a dot.
  • ◉Drag a bubble onto a dot and rest — that board slides in behind it. Let go to file it there.
  • ⏱Its own week — errands go red in 7 days, projects in a month.

Staying out of the way

A pill that
opens on a hover.

It rests as a small pill showing one number — red for what's overdue. It dims when unattended and brightens as you approach. Rest on it for a moment and it opens; leave and it folds back. Hover never steals your keyboard.

  • ⏳180 ms dwell to open, 450 ms grace before folding back — so passing over it does nothing.
  • 🫧Menu bar icon shows the overdue count across every board. Click to open; right-click for the menu.
  • ⌥⌥Space from anywhere shows or hides it.

Sound

Five pops.
Never the same twice.

Each burst plays one of five recorded bubble pops at random — never the same one twice running, because repetition is what makes a sound effect grating. Bursts overlap rather than cutting each other off. Tap one.

  Tap a bubble to hear it

Pops by Universfield via Pixabay. The speaker button in the bar mutes them, and that's remembered.
